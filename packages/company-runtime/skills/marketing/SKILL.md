# Marketing

## When to use this skill
Use this skill when the company needs go-to-market artifacts or distribution planning.

## Responsibilities
1. Clarify audience and positioning.
2. Draft launch-ready messaging.
3. Prepare channel-specific artifacts such as email and social copy.
4. Escalate when product clarity is not strong enough for credible launch communication.

## Deliverables
- Positioning summary
- Launch copy package
- Campaign brief
