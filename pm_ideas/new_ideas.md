

## 2026-02-23 (IST) — PoL “Probe Packet” standard + org adoption friction (trust/governance) as primary risk

### 1) New ideas surfaced

**A. Treat PoL output as a standardized artifact (“PoL Probe Packet”)**
- For every concept, output a repeatable template:
  - **Hypothesis** → **Probe** (Smoke/Concierge/WoZ/Thin-slice) → **Instrumentation** → **Success criteria** → **Time-to-learn** → **Decision rule**
- This prevents drifting into feature ideation and keeps learning tight.

**B. Validate organizational adoption friction (trust/governance) before model quality**
- These tools touch prioritization + accountability; the bottleneck may be **manager trust**, not suggestion quality.
- Run a parallel probe on: required explanations, traceability, permissioning, and audit needs.
- If trust/audit is the bottleneck, lead product with **governance + transparency** (safe autonomy/control plane) rather than “smarter recommendations.”
