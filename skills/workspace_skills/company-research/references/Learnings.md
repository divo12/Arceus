## Learning (2026-02-22)

- **Table stakes are converging** on: summarize + draft + rewrite + basic workspace Q&A embedded in core PM tools.
- **Differentiators are moving up-stack**:
  - *Atlassian Rovo*: strong on **unified search/chat/agents** across Atlassian products with **permissions-aware** access and third-party integration; plus Jira-native AI for issue creation/updates and automation.
  - *Notion AI*: emphasizes **enterprise search across apps**, **custom agents via MCP**, **multiple model support**, and **security/no training** posture.
  - *Aha! AI*: focuses on **embedded assistant across suite**, **prompt library/agents**, **admin controls**, and **usage/credits** management.
  - *Linear (positioning)*: leans into **triage intelligence** (e.g., similar issues) and AI-assisted workflows (details limited due to source fetch issues).
  - *Coda/Productboard (positioning)*: AI to **draft/spec** and **search insights** (details limited due to source fetch issues).
- **Big opportunities** for a new/expanded AI PM assistant:
  1. **Traceable product reasoning**: every summary/spec/prioritization output includes citations + “why” chain.
  2. **Cross-tool synchronization agent**: keeps backlog, roadmap, docs, and stakeholder updates consistent with approvals and rollback.
  3. **Prioritization copilot with explicit models**: integrates impact sizing, confidence, dependencies, and post-launch outcome tracking.
  4. **Enterprise-grade evaluation + governance**: prompt/agent test suites, audit logs, policy controls, and measurable ROI dashboards.
