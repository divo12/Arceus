## Elon Musk and SpaceX

**Context:** Musk wanted to send a rocket to Mars. Quotes for purchasing a rocket: up to $65M.

**First principles move:**
- What is a rocket made of? Aerospace-grade aluminum alloys, titanium, copper, carbon fiber.
- What is the commodity market value of those materials? ~2% of the typical rocket price.
- Conclusion: Build from raw materials instead of buying finished rockets.

**Outcome:** SpaceX; 10x cost reduction while profitable.

## Elon Musk and Battery Packs

**Conventional view:** "Battery packs cost $600/kWh and that's just how it is."

**First principles move:**
- What are the material constituents? Cobalt, nickel, aluminum, carbon, polymers, steel can.
- London Metal Exchange spot prices for each?
- Total: ~$80/kWh.
- Conclusion: The gap is manufacturing and combination, not materials. "Think of clever ways to take those materials and combine them into the shape of a battery cell."

## John Boyd's Snowmobile

**Setup:** Motorboat, military tank, bicycle.

**Deconstruct:**
- Motorboat: motor, hull, skis
- Tank: treads, armor, gun
- Bicycle: handlebars, wheels, gears, seat

**Reconstruct:** Snowmobile = handlebars + seat (bike) + treads (tank) + motor + skis (boat).

**Insight:** Unrelated fields, combined at the level of parts. Deconstruct then reconstruct.

## The Rolling Suitcase

**Context:** For millennia, bags and wheels existed. Romans had leather bags and wheeled vehicles. No one combined them until 1970.

**Why?** Innovators optimized *form* (better bags: zippers, nylon, backpacks vs suitcases). Bernard Sadow optimized *function* (store and move things efficiently). He saw a worker rolling a machine on a wheeled skid at an airport.

**Insight:** Optimize function. Ignore form.

## BuzzFeed and Distribution

**Conventional view:** SEO, editorial quality, traditional journalism.

**First principle of online success:** Distribution. Content that gets shared reaches people.

**Peretti's move:** Make content humans want to share. Ignore SEO. Obsess over virality, A/B testing, shareability.

**First principles of virality (Jon Steinberg):** Short, human aspect, chance to engage, authentic, images and lists, persuasive headline.

## CD Baby and Happy Customers

**Conventional view:** Need funding, offices, scale, "the best" systems.

**First principle:** A business needs happy customers to survive.

**Sivers' move:** Thrill each customer. No funding, minimal overhead, handmade systems. Famous order confirmation email (satin pillow, sterilized gloves). Focus entirely on existing customers.

**Outcome:** $4M/month revenue. "The way to grow your business is to focus entirely on your existing customers."

## Gutenberg's Printing Press

**Context:** Movable type existed for centuries. Improvement had been incremental.

**First principles move:** What are the constituent parts of printing? Type, paper, ink, and a way to apply pressure. The screw press (wine-making) could apply pressure. Combine screw press + movable type + paper + ink.

**Outcome:** Printing press. World-changing distribution of information.
