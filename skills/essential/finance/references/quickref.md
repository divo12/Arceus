# Finance Metrics Quick Reference

One-page lookup for essential SaaS & product finance metrics.

| Metric | Formula | Why PMs Care | Benchmark |
|--------|---------|--------------|-----------|
| Revenue | Total sales before expenses | Every feature should connect to revenue | Growth rate matters |
| Gross Margin | (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue × 100 | High-margin features > low-margin | SaaS: 70-85% |
| CAC | S&M Spend / New Customers | Shapes go-to-market strategy | Varies by model |
| LTV | ARPU × Avg Lifetime | What you can afford to spend on acquisition | 3x+ CAC |
| Churn | Customers Lost / Starting Customers | Silent killer of SaaS | Monthly: <2% great, 5%+ crisis |
| ARPU | Revenue / Total Users | Revenue per customer | Track trend |
| ARPA | MRR / Active Accounts | Account-level pricing | B2B: $100-$10K+ |
| ACV | Annual recurring per contract | Deal size, excludes one-time | SMB $5K-$25K; Enterprise $100K+ |
| MRR/ARR | MRR × 12 = ARR | Heartbeat of subscription | 5-10x valuation multiples |
| Burn/Runway | Spend - Revenue; Cash / Burn | What you can build | 12+ mo good; <6 crisis |
| LTV:CAC | LTV / CAC | Growth sustainability | 3:1 healthy; <1:1 unsustainable |
| Payback | CAC / (Monthly ARPU × Gross Margin %) | Cash efficiency | <12 mo great; >24 concerning |
| NRR | (Start + Expansion - Churn - Contraction) / Start | Revenue retention + expansion | >120% excellent; <90% problem |
| Contribution Margin | (Revenue - All Variable Costs) / Revenue | True contribution | 60-80% good |
| Rule of 40 | Growth % + Profit Margin % | Growth vs. efficiency | >40 healthy |
| Magic Number | (Q Rev - Prev Q Rev) × 4 / Prev Q S&M | S&M efficiency | >0.75 efficient |
| Quick Ratio | (New + Expansion) / (Churn + Contraction) | Gains vs. losses | >4 excellent; <2 leaky |

## Quick Decision Framework

**Feature investment:** Revenue impact? Margin impact? CAC/LTV impact? ROI >3x?

**Pricing change:** ARPU impact? Conversion/churn impact? NRR impact?

**GTM investment:** CAC? LTV? Payback? Magic Number? Scalability?

**Business health:** Growth rate? NRR? Quick Ratio? Rule of 40? Burn vs. runway?
