

## Update — 2026-02-23 04:10 IST (2-week MVP scope: Decision Packets happy path)

### 1) New ideas surfaced

- Anchor the 2-week MVP around **one happy path**:
  **create → collaborate → decide → publish → track outcome**.
- Treat **Decision Packet** as a product object with a lifecycle:
  **Draft → In Review → Decided → Archived**.
- MVP should prioritize **auditability + clarity** over “intelligence” (AI autopilot can come later).

**Critical choice to validate:** primary persona
- **Decision Driver** (pushes decision): fast creation, reviewer assignment, nudges, deadlines.
- **Approver/Reviewer** (exec): concise summary, risks/options, one-click decide, comment-only review.

Pick one for MVP to avoid building two half-products.
